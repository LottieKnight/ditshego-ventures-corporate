# Sovereign Brand Graphics Manifest

## 1. Core Assets
- **Primary Logo:** /images/logo.svg (Gold Metallic Sovereign Seal)
- **Favicons:** 
  - Root: logo.svg (1:1)
  - AURA: logo-aura.svg (Gold + Blue Glow)
  - SCHOOL: logo-school.svg (Gold + Pearl Glow)

## 2. Visual Language (Graphic Directives)
- **Backgrounds:** Deep radial gradients from #1a1a1a to #0a0a0a.
- **Borders:** 1px solid #333 with gold-accented hover states.
- **Imagery Style:**
  - **AURA:** High-contrast, blueprint-style diagrams, wireframes, and dark-mode data visualizations.
  - **SCHOOL:** Clean, minimalist layouts, high-key educational imagery with gold accents.
  - **Corporate:** High-end architectural photography, abstract geometric shapes in gold.

## 3. Component Standards
- **Sovereign Cards:** #1a1a1a bg, 12px radius, gold-left border.
- **Sovereign Buttons:** Gold bg, Onyx text, 4px radius.
