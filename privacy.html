Privacy policy.

How the house handles the limited personal information it encounters from corporate-site visitors — written to comply with POPIA.

POPIALast updated 30 August 2026
Last updated: 30 August 2026 · Ditshego Ventures (Pty) Ltd

Who we are

Ditshego Ventures (Pty) Ltd, registration number CIPC 2026/349873/07, registered in South Africa. This policy covers ditshego-ventures.co.za and its subdomains. The SCHOOL platform at school.ditshego-ventures.co.za has its own privacy and terms controls within that platform.

What we collect

  • Contact enquiries — name, email address, and the contents of any message you send us through the contact page or directly.
  • Technical data — standard, anonymised web analytics.

We do not sell personal information. We do not run third-party ad trackers on this site.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry — normally within two business days.
  • To carry out a partnership, investment or collaboration conversation you started.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Lawful basis (POPIA)

We process personal information on the basis of consent (you contacted us), the performance of a relationship you initiated, and legitimate interests — kept narrow and minimal.

Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except where necessary to operate our email and hosting (both South African and international providers), or where the law requires it.

Retention

Enquiry data is kept only for as long as needed to serve you, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact our Information Officer at moloti@ditshego-ventures.co.za. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa).

We will update this policy as the site evolves, and date every update.
