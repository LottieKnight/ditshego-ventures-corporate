Privacy policy.

How the house handles the limited personal information it encounters from corporate-site visitors — written to comply with POPIA.

POPIAVersion 1.0
Effective: 9 October 2026 · Version 1.0 · POPIA 4 of 2013

Effective Date: 9 October 2026 · Version: 1.0

Summary: Ditshego Ventures (Pty) Ltd operates a corporate website that is primarily informational. It collects little to no personal information from visitors. We do not sell data. This policy complies with South Africa's POPIA and explains your rights.

1. Who this applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to visitors of ditshego-ventures.co.za and its subpages, as well as anyone who contacts the house by email or phone. It does not apply to the SCHOOL platform (school.ditshego-ventures.co.za), which has its own policy.

2. The responsible party (POPIA)

DITSHEGO VENTURES (Pty) Ltd · Registration: 2026/349873/07 · 273 Ferguson Crescent, Eersterus, Pretoria, 0022, Gauteng, South Africa. Email: legal@ditshego-ventures.co.za.

Information Officer (POPIA s55): MK Ditshego — information-officer@ditshego-ventures.co.za.

3. What we collect

  • Voluntary contact data — your name, email address, and message when you email us or phone us.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, and standard server logs, used to keep the site secure and functioning.
  • No accounts, no cookies beyond essentials — this corporate site does not create accounts and sets no tracking, marketing, or advertising cookies.

4. How we use personal information

  • To respond to your enquiries (legitimate interest);
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations, including POPIA and PAIA (legal obligation);
  • To protect the security and integrity of the site (legitimate interest).

5. Third parties we share data with

  • Vercel, Inc. — hosting and content delivery (United States / global edge).
  • Google LLC — Google Fonts (browser is served the font files directly; no visitor data is shared by us).

We do not sell personal information. All processors are bound by data protection agreements.

6. Retention

Contact data is retained for as long as needed to respond to and follow up on the enquiry, and thereafter no longer than 2 years. Server and security logs are retained for 1 year.

7. Your rights

  • Access — request a copy of personal information we hold about you;
  • Correction — correct inaccurate information;
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal information;
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • Complain — lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (inforeg@justice.gov.za).

We respond to requests within 30 days. Contact the Information Officer at information-officer@ditshego-ventures.co.za.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to email and hosting systems is restricted and password-protected. In the event of a security compromise affecting personal information, we will notify the Information Regulator and you within 72 hours as required by POPIA s22.

9. Children

This is a corporate website. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes

Material changes will be published on this page and, where practical, notified by email. Continued use of the site after the new effective date constitutes acceptance.
