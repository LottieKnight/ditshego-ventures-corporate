AURA AI Laboratories.

Intelligence infrastructure for the Group and the continent — research, models, and tooling built in and for Africa.

Roadmap · 2026

Mandate

AURA exists to build the intelligence layer the Group and the continent will run on: applied AI research, model and evaluation tooling, and infrastructure that respects local context, cost, and data constraints.

Why it belongs to the house

Every venture in the portfolio benefits from shared intelligence capability. AURA is where that capability is built once and compounded — the Ludo Principle applied to engineering. It also returns strength to SCHOOL, whose AI tutor depends on this layer.

Current status

A roadmap for 2026 is being assembled. Governance, compute, and partnering decisions remain open.

What's next

  • Publish the research roadmap and programme areas
  • Establish compute and evaluation foundations
  • Define the first applied research projects with SCHOOL

Registration and structure details for AURA AI Laboratories will be published as they formalise. For enquiries, the line is open at moloti@ditshego-ventures.co.za.
