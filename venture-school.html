One platform, every learner

SCHOOL connects learners to the full stack of what studying in South Africa actually requires: past papers, an AI tutor that knows your campus, module rooms where classmates answer real questions, a study planner, a daily quiz, bursaries, and opportunity. One login, no barriers, no conditions.

It is data-light by design — built for South African connections. No heavy video, no forced downloads. The cost of what sits behind the platform is the house's to carry, not the learner's.
