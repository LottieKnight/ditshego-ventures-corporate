Getting Started NPC.

An entrepreneurial incubator for South African youth — structured as a non-profit company.

Formalising

Mandate

Getting Started NPC gives young South Africans the first-move advantage: incubation, mentorship, and structure for early-stage ideas. It exists so that opportunity is created, not waited for.

Why it belongs to the house

The house funds it and the portfolio feeds it — SCHOOL supplies the learners, SCENE the stories, the group the experience. Philanthropy that returns capability to the country is a piece of the board, not a footnote.

Current status

Formalisation of the NPC is underway, including governance and registration steps.

What's next

  • Complete NPC registration and governance documents
  • Define the incubator cohort model
  • Secure the first founding partners

Registration and structure details for Getting Started NPC will be published as they formalise. For enquiries, the line is open at moloti@ditshego-ventures.co.za.
